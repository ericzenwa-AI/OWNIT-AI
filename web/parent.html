What it does

When a student is stuck on a maths question, the problem is usually not the topic the question is about. It is something underneath it, often from years earlier, that never quite set.

ownIT asks a handful of short questions, working down one layer at a time, until it finds the first thing that gives way. Then it teaches that one thing — step by step, with your child doing the working — and brings them back to the question they were stuck on.

What it will not do

It never gives them the answer to their homework. That is deliberate. An answer gets them through tonight; they are stuck again next week on the same thing wearing a different hat. They can show the result to a teacher without it looking like they cheated, because they didn't.
